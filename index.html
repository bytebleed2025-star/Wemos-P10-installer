P10 flashing note

The P10 buzzer is wired to RX / GPIO3. Unplug the buzzer before flashing over USB, otherwise the serial upload can fail. Use a USB data cable; if the board does not enter boot mode, hold its BOOT button while clicking Install.

After flashing

P10: join GAME_STOP WATCH and open http://192.168.4.1 (web password bytebleeder).
P5: join P5 MATRIX and open http://192.168.4.1 or p5matrix.local / p5video.local.
